Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding booking, travel dates, and payment options.

How do I book a tour package with Chillz Tour?
You can select any package from our Tour Packages page and click "View Details" to submit a booking form, or directly contact our team via WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210. Our travel experts will confirm availability, customize the itinerary if needed, and issue your booking voucher.
Can I customize the day-wise itinerary and hotels?
Yes! All our domestic tour packages are 100% customizable. You can upgrade hotel ratings, extend stay durations, add specific sightseeing spots, or include private transfers.
What is included in the package price?
Most packages include hotel/resort accommodations, daily breakfast, private AC vehicle transfers, sightseeing tours, and applicable taxes. Specific inclusions like flight/train tickets or meals are clearly mentioned on each package detail page.
What are the cancellation and refund policies?
We offer flexible cancellation terms. Full refunds are provided for cancellations made up to 15 days prior to travel date (excluding non-refundable peak resort charges). Detailed terms are shared during booking confirmation.
